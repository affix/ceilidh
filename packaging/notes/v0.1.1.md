## Smaller downloads

The bundled artwork was being shipped at far higher resolution than anything draws it. Every piece is now stored at the largest size the game actually asks for, worked out from the playfield layout at 4K, and quantised to a 256 colour palette.

| | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Artwork and icon | 12.5 MB | 940 KB |
| Debian packages | 21 MB | about 11 MB |

Nothing looks different on screen. The arrows peak at 291px even on a 4K display and are stored at 512px, so there is still headroom above anything the game will request.
