## Calibration that lands on time

Calibration had the sign of the global offset backwards. The offset is added to song time, so adding the measured lag made the song read as later still, and stepping 50 ms late, calibrating and playing left every step judged 100 ms late. It now subtracts the error instead.

The calibration click also plays straight from the mixer and never goes through the song clock, so what it measures is the whole latency, not a correction on top of the current offset. Calibrating now sets the offset from the measurement outright, so running it a second time no longer counts the first run twice.
